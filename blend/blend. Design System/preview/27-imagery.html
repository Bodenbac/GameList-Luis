Do

  • Natural north-window daylight, soft and indirect
  • Hands pouring, mixing, carrying. Never staring faces
  • Off-center editorial framing, generous negative space
  • Cream / sage / deep green / soft amber grading
  • Subtle film grain, light touch
  • Real shadows, real mess on the counter

Don't

  • Studio key lights, flash, vignettes
  • White-background product cutouts
  • Smiling families clinking glasses
  • Color-blocked or gradient backgrounds
  • Glassmorphism, isometric 3D, AI-generated fruit
  • Plastic-perfect produce, oversaturation
"Sunday afternoon, kitchen counter, sun through a north window."
